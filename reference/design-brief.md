Design a premium informative website based on the uploaded PDF. The website should transform the document into an engaging digital experience while maintaining a refined editorial aesthetic inspired by museum exhibitions, cultural heritage websites, and high-end digital publications.

The objective is to elevate the presentation, not rewrite the content.

🚨 Highest Priority: Preserve the PDF Wording

The uploaded PDF is the single source of truth.
Use the exact wording from the PDF throughout the website.
Under no circumstances should you:

* rewrite the content
* paraphrase
* summarize
* simplify
* translate
* shorten
* expand
* reorder sentences
* replace words with synonyms
* invent additional information
* generate replacement headings
* alter the meaning or intent of any sentence

Every heading, paragraph, bullet point, quotation, citation, reference, numbering, and label should preserve the exact wording from the PDF.

The wording must remain word-for-word identical.

However, you may adjust formatting for presentation purposes, including:
* capitalization (e.g. converting ALL CAPS headings to Title Case when appropriate)
* punctuation if necessary for visual presentation
* line breaks
* typography
* spacing
* visual hierarchy
* grouping content into cards
* accordions
* tabs
* timelines
* comparison panels
* responsive layouts
* decorative elements
* illustrations

These formatting changes must never alter the wording, sentence structure, or meaning of the original content.
Think of this project as republishing the PDF as a premium digital editorial website. The design changes, but the writing does not.
